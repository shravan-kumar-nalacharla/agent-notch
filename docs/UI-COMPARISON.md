## Intentional improvements

- The purple mascot was replaced by eyes-only white-on-dark and dark-on-light states.
- The closed island is a 150 x 30 flush notch with natural blinking and cursor-following gaze.
- Expanded content is 620 px wide with a 1,000 ms cancelable close grace period.
- All five primary modules share one header, arrow navigation and five-dot pager.
- Light and dark themes use shared tokens.
- Native window bounds animate at approximately 60 updates per second instead of switching abruptly.
- Empty states never show fabricated reminder counts, progress, media timing or tasks.
