# Agent Notch 0.17.1 — player, settings and interruption fixes

Settings opens and focuses after its first load using ready-to-show, as a child of the topmost notch. A single trusted pointer click is covered by the Windows fixture.

Music now has a separate full-width seek bar, an output waveform next to the track heading, no playhead stick inside the waveform, and a short system-volume slider at the left. Playback controls stay mounted. Synchronized lyrics show only the current sung line (18px bold) and the preceding line (smaller and faded). Future lines stay hidden until their timestamps; plain lyrics without timestamps are not presented as synchronized. Track identity changes trigger lookup; unrelated player-list updates do not. Lookup generations reject stale completions.

During an assistant answer, the local Windows wake recognizer can accept stop / stop speaking / please stop, hide / hide yourself, and quit. Stop cancels pending voice work and playback while holding the panel open. Hide and quit collapse the notch through the original animation; quit here means dismiss the panel, not exit the application. Explicit activation opens it again. This hands-free path uses the already opted-in Always listening mode. Push-to-Talk continues to capture only while held; recognized control requests use the same stop/hide path. Muting still releases recognition and blocks activation. Timers, music and meeting recordings are not cancelled by voice interruption.

The next cloud speech segment is prefetched during playback to reduce sentence-boundary pauses. Cancellation aborts pending synthesis and prevents subsequent segments playing. No provider, credentials or language selection is changed.

The 113-second supplied video was reviewed as a whole contact sheet and at 10fps around the lyric flicker. The recording confirms repeated Looking for lyrics states and future lines showing early.

Validation: 274 unit tests plus a separate playback lifecycle test pass. Windows source and packaged fixtures pass one-click Settings, stop/hide behavior, player layout and timed lyric visibility (5), voice panel and original motion behavior (12), actual PTT hook/capture with synthetic Sarvam service (6), and microphone privacy/persistence (13). Native English recognition tests recognized synthesized stop, hide and quit with confidence above 0.94; muted activation remained blocked. Physical interruption during speaker playback remains a user verification item because room acoustics and echo affect recognition.
